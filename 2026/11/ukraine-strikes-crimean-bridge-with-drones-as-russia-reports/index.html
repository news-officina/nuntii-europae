Ukraine strikes Crimean bridge with drones as Russia reports record drone interceptions

Sergei Aksyonov, the Kremlin-backed head of Crimea, said road traffic would be restricted until at least midday. Russia's Defense Ministry said it intercepted 1,336 drones, the highest number since the 2022 invasion began.

16 November 2026

The Armed Forces of Ukraine attacked the Crimean bridge with drones, and Sergei Aksyonov, the Kremlin-backed head of Crimea, said road traffic would be restricted until at least midday. Russia's Defense Ministry said it intercepted 1,336 Ukrainian drones over Russian regions and Crimea, the highest number since the 2022 full-scale invasion began. In Ukraine, power company DTEK said Russian attacks left 50,000 people without electricity in the southern Odesa region.

President Volodymyr Zelensky of Ukraine said the United States had proposed that Kyiv cede some territory in exchange for being given NATO membership. On the Russian side, Deputy Prime Minister Marat Khusnullin said an assessment of damage to the Crimean bridge after the attack was under way. He said the timeframe for its full restoration would be clear soon.

The drone warhead struck the right arch of the bridge on the Crimean side, a detail confirmed on the morning of 16 November. Rail traffic was also disrupted: the operator Grand Service Express said four Tavria trains were delayed by between two and 4.5 hours because of the overnight closure.

In the Belgorod region, a Ukrainian drone killed a truck driver, local authorities said. Drone debris fell across 19 addresses in the southern Krasnodar region and injured at least six people, two of them minors, according to local authorities. Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov said: "Unfortunately, no system in the world can provide 100 percent protection against such large-scale strikes on various targets."

Express said rail traffic on the railway part of the bridge resumed as of 9:00 on 16 November, according to RIA Novosti. Senator Vladimir Dzhabarov called the strike an attempt by the Ukrainian authorities to derail negotiations on settling the conflict. The bridge was struck before, in October 2022, when a truck bomb detonated on it and caused months of disruption.
