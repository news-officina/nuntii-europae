US says Europe will join next Ukraine talks as EU argues over who represents it

Jared Kushner, the US envoy, said Moscow and Kyiv had agreed to bring European countries in, and Russia and the US want Europe to name one person to deal with Russia. Kaja Kallas and António Costa have put themselves forward.

13 November 2026

Ukraine and Russia have agreed to bring European countries into the next round of peace negotiations, Jared Kushner, the US envoy of Donald Trump, said late on 12 November. Kushner said President Zelenskyy had been a very strong supporter of the move. Russia and the United States are now pressing Europe to name a single representative they can deal with.

Kaja Kallas, the EU's foreign policy chief, has pitched herself, alongside European Council President António Costa, to represent the EU in the Ukraine negotiations. She argued that the decision on who should deal with Russia was already settled when she was offered her post in 2024. Luxembourg's Prime Minister Luc Frieden backs Costa. "We, as heads of government, have a representative. And that is President Costa", he said.

Others want the pairing instead. Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni wrote to Costa on Thursday, calling for a single European representative with a clear negotiating mandate. In the letter, reported by Euronews and the Kyiv Post, she warned that Europe risked becoming irrelevant without a coordinated approach. "We need to identify who should represent European interests and with which mandate", she wrote. Estonia's Prime Minister Kristen Michal told Politico he backed Costa and Kaja Kallas.

Kushner and Steve Witkoff, another US envoy, held two days of talks with Russian and Ukrainian delegations in Abu Dhabi, which no European officials joined, before meeting President Putin in Moscow and President Zelenskyy at a military airbase outside Dublin. At the European Political Community meeting there, 43 countries signed a declaration stating that European security must be decided by Europeans.

According to the Kyiv Post, Witkoff and Kushner want this week's round in Abu Dhabi, however, to be the last without full European participation, and they aim to speed the talks up. Zelenskyy has urged the same, saying Europe must become a regular part of this negotiation.
